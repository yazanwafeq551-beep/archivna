## Concept

**«الوثيقة والذاكرة» — The Document and the Memory.** An open archival document
whose spine is the hidden Arabic alef «أ», whose upper-right corner dissolves
into shrinking digital pixels (digitization), framed by a subtle Jerusalem
horseshoe arch around the hamza, with an olive sprig growing from the seam and
three inner paper layers for past / present / future. See the manual for the
full narrative, rules, and motion spec.
